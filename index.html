Build it and flash it

Open a terminal on 4. Tutorial/ and run the launcher:

./launch.sh

Pick a lesson, then 1 to check what's missing, 2 to install a toolchain, 3 to unpack the libraries, 6 to build, flash and monitor. Press r to print every command for the lesson without running any of them — Test Code/ is only ever read, all output lands in .build/.

Before you flash: unplug the HC-06 — it shares D0/D1 with the USB-serial chip and the upload will fail at not in sync. From lesson 09 on, the motors spin the moment the board boots, so hold the car still and upright at power-on: setup() samples the gyro offset there, and moving it poisons the balance point.
